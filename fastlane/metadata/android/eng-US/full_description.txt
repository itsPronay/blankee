Blankee – Ambient Sounds for Focus, Relaxation & Sleep

Improve your focus, relax your mind, or fall asleep more easily with Blankee. Mix calming ambient sounds and create your perfect soundscape for work, study, meditation, relaxation, or sleep.

Choose from a variety of natural and relaxing sounds such as rain, ocean waves, fireplace, forest, wind, and more. You can combine multiple sounds, adjust their individual volumes, and create a sound environment that fits your mood.

Relaxing Ambient Sounds

Create your own atmosphere with a selection of ambient sounds, including:

• Rain
• Ocean waves
• Fireplace
• Forest
• Wind
• And more

Mix different sounds together to create your ideal background audio.

😴 Sleep Timer

Use the built-in sleep timer when you're getting ready for bed. Set how long you want the sounds to play and Blankee will automatically stop playback when the timer ends.

🎵 Custom Sounds

Want to use your own sounds? Add audio files from your device and include them in your mixes. Create a completely personalized soundscape using sounds you already love.

🎚️ Individual Volume Controls

Fine-tune every sound independently. Make the rain louder, keep the waves subtle, or balance several sounds exactly the way you like them.

 💾 Save Your Favorite Presets

Found the perfect combination? Save it as a preset and quickly return to your favorite soundscape whenever you need it.

 🌙 Dark & Light Mode

Choose the theme that feels most comfortable for you with full support for both dark and light modes.

🌍 Multilingual

Blankee supports 20 languages, making the app accessible to users around the world.

📱 Designed for Android

Blankee is designed to work beautifully across phones, tablets, and foldable devices with a responsive interface that adapts to different screen sizes.
